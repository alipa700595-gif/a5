Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2026 | Basil Broth Camp

1. Commitment to User Data Privacy

Basil Broth Camp ("we," "our," or "the Camp") operates basilbrothcamp.com. We are committed to protecting the personal privacy and data security of our students, patrons, and digital visitors in strict compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and international data protection standards.

2. Data We Collect

We collect only the essential personal information required to process workshop registrations, manage camp reservations, and deliver our culinary journal dispatches:

  • Direct Contact Data: Name, email address, phone number, dietary allergy notes, and billing information provided upon workshop registration.
  • Aggregated Technical Data: Anonymized IP addresses, browser versions, referring domains, and page interaction metrics collected via secure Google Analytics 4 (Tag ID: G-0LY0HY7L01).

3. Data Usage & Third-Party Processors

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third-party advertisers. Data is shared strictly with secure technical infrastructure providers (such as encrypted payment gateways and web hosting servers) necessary for camp operations.

4. Your Legal Rights

You have the right to request access to your personal data, rectify inaccurate records, or request complete erasure from our databases at any time by contacting our privacy officer.
