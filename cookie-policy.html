1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your browser that help us remember user preferences, maintain active navigation states, and compile aggregated traffic statistics.

2. Categories of Cookies We Use

  • Essential Cookies: Required for secure navigation, form submissions, and mobile responsiveness.
  • Analytics Cookies: Powered by Google Analytics 4 (G-0LY0HY7L01) to track overall visitor numbers and popular recipe pages without collecting personal identifiers.

3. Managing Cookie Preferences

You can configure your browser to reject or delete cookies through your browser security settings at any time.
