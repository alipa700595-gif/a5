Basil Broth Camp Gastronomic & Dietary Guidelines

1. Educational Purpose Only

The culinary recipes, botanical extraction guides, and foraging techniques published on basilbrothcamp.com are provided for educational and culinary appreciation purposes only. Content does not constitute formal medical or nutritional advice.

2. Foraging Safety & Identification

Wild mushroom and herb foraging requires experienced, verified identification. Never consume any wild plant or mushroom unless 100% verified by a certified professional mycologist or botanist.

3. Food Allergies & Preparation

While our camp accommodates diverse dietary preferences, patrons are responsible for communicating all personal food allergies prior to attending workshop sessions.
